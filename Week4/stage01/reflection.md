#Reflection

The SmartCare appointment system prototype explored how patient names, practitioner names, and appointment times could be displayed and stored. The enhanced version made use of a list, dictionaries, and functions, and through this process a few limitations became clear, including gaps in validation and the possibility of double bookings.

Microsoft Copilot was then brought in as a tutor to explain the code, point out potential issues, and offer suggestions for improvements. A number of these suggestions were genuinely helpful, particularly those relating to validation, although the AI also made assumptions at times meaning not every suggestion could be accepted.

To verify the code, it was tested across several scenario including empty values, none values, and a double booking scenario. Ultimately, the responsibility remained mine to understand what the task required, critically assess the AI’s suggestions, test them thoroughly, determine what was genuinely appropriate, and making sure I could actually explain the code myself.
